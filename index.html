NEXORA AI is an intelligent workspace that helps you research, write, organize ideas, summarize information, and automate repetitive tasks in one place.

Can I use it for studying?

Absolutely. The Study Assistant turns your notes into clear explanations, quizzes, and revision material.

Does NEXORA AI store my data?

Your projects and conversations are stored securely so you can pick up right where you left off, across every device.

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings.
